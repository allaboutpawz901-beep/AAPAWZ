1. Catalog Overview
This catalog presents a curriculum framework for 15 veterinary and animal care pathways. Each pathway has been pedagogically sequenced to guide learners from foundational knowledge through clinical practice and experiences. Course descriptions, instructional hours, prerequisite structures, and compliance alignments are provided for every course across all pathways.
The curriculum is designed to align with the competency areas identified in the program compliance matrix, which references standards from recognized veterinary and animal care professional organizations. Final curriculum should be reviewed against the current official standards of each accrediting or certifying body before submission for accreditation or certification approval.
2. Pedagogical Sequencing Model
All pathways follow a five-phase pedagogical progression that arranges courses in the most practical learning sequence:
Phase 1: Foundations
Introductory courses establishing core knowledge, terminology, safety, and professional orientation. Learners build the vocabulary and conceptual frameworks required for all subsequent coursework.
Phase 2: Core Skills
Essential clinical or technical skills courses building on foundational knowledge. Learners develop hands-on competencies in handling, basic procedures, and primary care.
Phase 3: Applied Practice
Intermediate courses applying core skills to clinical scenarios. Learners integrate multiple skill sets in realistic contexts and develop diagnostic reasoning.
Phase 4: /Specialty
 coursework in specialized areas. Learners master complex procedures, specialty disciplines, and diagnostic or therapeutic techniques.
Phase 5: /Clinical
Integrative courses, clinical practicums, and examination review. Learners demonstrate competency through supervised practice and portfolio assessment.
Sequencing Rule: Safety and professional orientation courses are placed first, followed by anatomy/behavior/handling, then procedures/nursing/laboratory/diagnostics, then specialty/management topics, and finally practicum//review.
3. Term and Milestone Framework
Each pathway is organized into up to four terms, with completion milestones at 2, 6, 12, and 24 months. Shorter programs may complete in fewer terms, while programs extend across all four terms.
Milestone Definitions:
2-Month Milestone
Certificate of Completion — Foundational competency achieved. Graduate has mastered terminology, safety, and basic skills.
6-Month Milestone
Certificate of Proficiency — Core competency achieved. Graduate has completed core clinical or technical skill coursework.
12-Month Milestone
Certificate of Practice — Applied competency achieved. Graduate has completed practice and specialty coursework.
24-Month Milestone
Program Completion — Full competency achieved. Graduate has completed all program requirements including /practicum and is prepared for certification or employment.
Note: Not all pathways include all four milestones. Program duration depends on pathway scope and complexity.

Address potential cost issue
